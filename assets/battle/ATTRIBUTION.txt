Battle character pipeline
-------------------------
- Runtime: Esoteric Spine (spine_flutter) — industry-standard 2D skeletal animation
- Runtime: Rive (rive) — interactive state-machine animation
- Runtime: Lottie (lottie) — After Effects / Bodymovin exports
- Photoreal pose plates: Catdex-generated key poses (idle/scratch/pounce/special)
  packed as Spine atlas regions for cat_tabby / cat_black
- Example skeletons (owl/raptor/dragon): Esoteric spine-runtimes examples
- gray64 pixel pack: OpenGameArt "Gray cat asset pack" (CC0) by skristi
